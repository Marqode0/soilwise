1. POSITION AND DUTIES

The Employee shall serve as a Lab Technician and shall perform the following duties:

  • Receive and log incoming soil samples from collection agents
  • Conduct soil testing for NPK, pH, and organic matter content
  • Upload accurate test results to the SoilWise platform
  • Maintain lab equipment and ensure proper calibration
  • Follow all safety and quality control protocols
2. COMPENSATION
  • Monthly Salary: ₦150,000 (One Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira Only)
  • Bonus per Sample: ₦10,000 per soil sample tested
  • Payment Date: Last working day of every month
3. WORKING HOURS
  • 40 hours per week (flexible schedule)
  • Work may be required on weekends for urgent samples
4. CONTRACT DURATION
  • Initial contract: 12 months
  • Renewable based on performance
5. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Employee agrees to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all soil test results, farmer data, and company operations.

6. EQUIPMENT AND SUPPLIES

All lab equipment, testing supplies, and personal protective equipment (PPE) will be provided by SoilWise.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT

The Employee shall adhere to SoilWise's ethical guidelines and professional standards at all times.

8. TERMINATION
  • Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' notice
  • Immediate termination for gross misconduct

By signing below, the Employee agrees to all terms and conditions stated above.
